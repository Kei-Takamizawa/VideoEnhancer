What this mockup shows

  • Opens on thumbnail click: the poster shows at once and playback starts on the same click. Close with X, then click a thumbnail to try it.
  • Controls float on the video with no dark panel behind them, so the picture shows through. Thin shadows keep icons and text readable.
  • Controls, title and cursor hide after 2 s while playing. Any mouse move brings them back. Paused: always visible.
  • Before / After splits the picture. Nothing is drawn on the divider. Use the slider or a two-finger horizontal scroll.
  • Original is softer, blocky and 30 fps. Enhanced is sharp and 60 fps. While paused, the step buttons move one output frame.
  • Hovering the seek bar shows a preview above the bar, never over it, with the time under the picture.
  • Ctrl + scroll (pinch) zooms 100 to 800% around the cursor. Two-finger scroll pans when zoomed.
  • Details opens a sidebar. Its delete and remove buttons only show a note here.
  • Press ? in the player for all shortcuts. Double-click the video for fullscreen.
  • The strip above the player (theme, sample shape, status chip) is for this mockup only.
